# Third-party data

## JLPT vocabulary lists

The JLPT N5 to N1 vocabulary lists (`KotobaMichi-Frontend/src/data/vocab-tanos.ts`
and the imports in `KotobaMichi-Backend/csv-imports/`) are based on the lists by
Jonathan Waller at [tanos.co.uk/jlpt](https://www.tanos.co.uk/jlpt/), licensed
under [Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
The lists were reformatted and extended with topics and Sinhala glosses.
